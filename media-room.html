Signal plan

rev B · PS5 moved to desk

Both machines now live at the desk. The PS5 stays where it already is, under the desk next to the PC — because you want to play it on a monitor sometimes, and a 1 m cable to the monitor's second input is the whole solution. For big-screen sessions, each machine gets its own fiber-optic HDMI run to the TV. Two cables in one trunking channel, no switches, no splitters — switches are exactly where 4K120 handshakes go to die, and a second cable costs less than a good one.

Two side-effects, both good. The credenza now only houses the receiver, so it can be smaller. And the receiver never touches a PC or console signal, so the whole category of AVR handshake trouble stays gone.

The privacy constraint

You don't want the monitors readable from the doorway. That is a layout rule, not a gadget: the desk goes on the same wall as the door, or the wall the door swings toward, so the door opens onto the backs of the monitors. Which wall that is comes out of the photos — flag it when you shoot them, and the layout drawing gets built around it. Belt-and-braces: set Windows to lock on a short timer, so what's on screen is gone before anyone is through the door anyway.

How to order

3 lanes
Lane 1 · days

KSA stock

Amazon.sa, noon, eXtra, Jarir. Next-day to a few days, local warranty, easy returns. The TV, receiver, most speakers, and all cables and lighting live here. Order these any time.

Lane 2 · 1–2 weeks

GCC ships to KSA

Samma3a, PULT (UAE), Dr.Head (UAE), Amazon.ae. Fills the gaps KSA stock doesn't carry — the SVS sub and KEF in-ceilings mainly. Shipping is cheap within the Gulf.

Lane 3 · 2–4 weeks

B&H import

Widest audio selection, often cheaper before fees — then add ~15% VAT plus customs and freight on arrival, and warranty service means shipping it back to the US. Use only for what lanes 1–2 can't source. December deadline: order lane 3 items first.

Order of operations

Dec deadline

Sequence matters more than speed

The wedding is December. Lane-3 imports and the acoustic quote are the long poles — start those first. The KSA-stock items arrive in days and can wait until the room is painted.

  1. Answered — the ceiling is hollow

    The photos show a gypsum tray ceiling with recessed lights and a built-in RGB cove: a generous void by construction. Order the in-ceiling speakers (longest lead item) and coordinate the two cutouts with the existing downlight pairs and cove strips.

  2. Now — get the acoustic quote

    Contact Acoustic.sa with the room dimensions for six panels + four corner traps. Custom fabrication has lead time, and their quote tells us if the 4,400 estimate holds.

  3. This month — order the sub and heights

    SVS from noon/Samma3a, in-ceilings per step 1. These are the only items with real shipping risk against December.

  4. When the room is painted — everything KSA-stocked

    TV, receiver, fronts, centre, surrounds, cables, lights. All of it arrives in under a week. Run the trunking and mount the TV before the furniture goes in.

  5. Last — calibrate

    Run the Denon's Audyssey mic from the actual seat after the rug and panels are in. Treatment first, calibration second — the mic corrects the room it hears.
