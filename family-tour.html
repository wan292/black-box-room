What goes in, piece by piece

المكونات

Ceiling speakers ×2 سماعات السقف

Flush in the tray, invisible until they rain on you. This is what makes Atmos real — helicopters overhead, rain from above. The hollow gypsum makes it an easy install.

placement math

Panels + bass traps المعالجة الصوتية

Black fabric panels at the reflection points, thick traps in the corners. Same material as inside your walls — but on the room side, so the sound inside stays clean instead of booming. Made in Riyadh.

acoustic.sa · MMT Acoustix

Walnut slat wall جدار الخشب

The one warm feature that stops black feeling like a cave. Goes on the back wall behind the sofa, on black felt — it looks boutique and scatters echo at the same time.

GCC panels

The big rug السجادة

The polished marble is beautiful and it is the room's worst enemy — it echoes and it mirrors the screen. A 250×350 wool rug with a felt pad underneath fixes both at once.

Blackout curtains الستائر المعتمة

Wall-to-wall on the window wall, in heavy dark fabric. Daylight control for movie afternoons — and the thick pleats quietly absorb sound too.

Star ceiling — optional سقف النجوم

Fiber-optic stars in the tray. Your hollow ceiling is the easy case for it — but it's a construction-phase job, so the family decides this one before the ceiling work, or never.
