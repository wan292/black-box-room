What goes in, piece by piece

الأغراض وحدة وحدة

Ceiling speakers ×2 سماعات السقف

A round speaker that sits flush inside the gypsum, invisible until it rains on you. This is what makes Atmos real — and your hollow tray ceiling makes it an easy job.

Acoustic panel لوحة عزل صوت

This is the whole product: a wood frame filled with rock wool, wrapped in black fabric, hung on the wall like a picture. It stops sound bouncing back at you — the room sounds clean instead of echoey. You need 6–8 of them, made in Riyadh.

acoustic.sa · MMT Acoustix

Bass trap مصيدة الباص

Same material as the panel but much thicker, and it stands in the corner — a tall soft column from floor to ceiling. Deep bass piles up in corners; this soaks it up so the subwoofer sounds tight, not boomy. Two corners minimum, behind the TV.

Wood slat wall — optional جدار خشب

Warm walnut strips on black felt, behind the sofa. It's decoration first — this one is your mother's call, and any direction she takes the room works with the plan. If she happens to like slats, the felt backing quietly helps the echo too.

amazon.sa slat panels

The big rug السجادة

A completely normal rug — nothing special to hunt for. Just: big (about 250×350), thick rather than flat-woven, wool if possible, and a felt pad underneath. The shiny marble echoes and mirrors the screen; the rug fixes both.

Curtains, double layer ستاير تعتيم

Exactly the setup you already have in your bedroom and love: blackout on the windows, and the big wall-to-wall curtain over it. Same thing here on the windows wall — darkness for movie afternoons, and the heavy pleats quietly absorb sound too.

Star ceiling — optional سقف النجوم

Fiber-optic stars in the ceiling tray. Your hollow ceiling is the easy case — but it means drilling the fresh gypsum, so the family decides this one before any ceiling work, or it stays a dream.

Your corner desk stays مكتبك زي ما هو

The 2×2 corner desk, three monitors, the NZXT and the Nanoleafs — all of it moves in as-is, into the corner by the door. Bonus: sitting near the TV wall means the PC-to-TV cable drops from 15 m to about 5 m. A console shelf under it can come later if you want one.
